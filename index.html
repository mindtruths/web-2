How it works

Built from the science. Personalized for you.

We started with the research: which behavioral and environmental factors keep depressive symptoms going, and which ones help build well-being. From there, we drew on gold-standard, well-validated treatments that target exactly those factors.

The result is a system that turns clinical science into suggestions that work on your schedule, for your life.

1

Start with the research

We dug into what's known about the behavioral and environmental factors that maintain depressive symptoms, and the ones that help promote well-being.

2

Draw from clinical best practice

We looked for gold-standard treatment approaches that target those specific factors and have been proven to work, and translated them into brief, actionable suggestions.

3

Personalize it to you

We learn your own patterns and abilities and use this to determine which suggestions might be most helpful for you.

4

Deliver it in real time

We give you suggestions at the moments when you might most benefit from them throughout your day, taking into consideration your schedule and lifestyle.

5

Track what's working

We track your progress using standardized measures that clinicians and scientists already use, so change is validated and visible.

6

Show you everything

We show your functioning across areas of your life and explain why we chose the suggestions we did, so you can understand your own patterns and see the whole picture.

Why Mindtruths

The week your patient lives in, visible to you.

Mindtruths gives your patient support during the other 167 hours of the week outside of your sessions. Invite a patient into Mindtruths from your dashboard to integrate their experience with your care and gain insight. Here's what that changes about what you can see and do.

Data from their actual week

Mindtruths collects information on mood, behaviors, and functioning throughout the 167 hours outside the session, not just what they remember to bring up.

Support between sessions

From their data, Mindtruths identifies which behavioral pathways might be load-bearing right now, and delivers just-in-time adaptive interventions (JITAIs) directly to the patient at the moments they're likely to matter.

You select the targets

Choose or override what Mindtruths is working on for each patient. It drives the day-to-day arc; you hold the wheel.

Full visibility into what they're seeing

Your dashboard shows exactly which insights and suggestions are being surfaced to your patient, not just a summary of them.

Progress on validated scales

Track progress on validated assessment scales like the PHQ-9 and GAD-7, alongside the behavioral data underneath the score.

Safety risks, flagged to you

If a patient's responses raise a safety risk, you're alerted directly. Nothing about safety gets softened or held for later.
